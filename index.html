01 / Market microstructure

Orderbook depth & impact

Resting limit orders form the book. A market order walks the ladder and its average fill price drifts away from mid. A larger order consumes more levels and pays more slippage.

02 / Perpetual basis

Funding rate TWAP & clamp

The funding rate follows a time-weighted premium of mark over index plus a baseline interest component, then gets clamped to a symmetric band. Longs pay shorts when funding is positive.

03 / Margin

Isolated vs cross liquidation

04 / Contagion

Liquidation cascade

A price shock marks every position to market. Undercollateralized positions are force-closed into the book; each closing order pushes the price further and can trigger the next round.
